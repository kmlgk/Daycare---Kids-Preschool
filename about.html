Our Story & Philosophy

Fifteen years of first steps, first words, and first friendships — built by a team that treats your child like family.

How We Started

Built by Parents, for Parents

Little Sprouts Early Learning opened its doors in 2011 when founder Ethan Wallace — then a first-time dad — couldn't find a daycare in Brookfield Heights that balanced genuine warmth with real academic structure. So he built one.

What began as a single toddler classroom has grown into a full campus serving infants through Pre-K, without losing the small-community feeling that made families trust us in the first place.

Today, our philosophy is simple: children learn best when they feel safe, loved, and free to explore. Every policy, every classroom layout, and every teacher we hire is measured against that standard.

Our Mission

To give every child a joyful, safe, and stimulating early start — and to give every family a partner they can trust completely with the people they love most.

Our Vision

To be the early-learning center every Brookfield Heights family thinks of first — known as much for academic outcomes as for the warmth felt the moment you walk in.

Quick Answers

A Few Things Families Often Ask

Q

What makes Little Sprouts different from other centers nearby?

A

Lower ratios than state minimums, degree-qualified lead teachers in every room, and a founder who still walks the halls daily. It's the small decisions, repeated for 15 years.

Q

Is Little Sprouts licensed and accredited?

A

Yes — we're a State of Illinois licensed childcare center with a 5-star quality rating, inspected annually.

Q

Can I tour before enrolling my child?

A

Always. Tours run most weekdays and include time with your child's future teacher — no pressure, just questions answered.
