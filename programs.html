Our Curriculum Philosophy

One Philosophy, Four Age-Appropriate Expressions

Every program below shares the same foundation — play-based learning, individualized pacing, and standards-aligned outcomes — just expressed differently depending on what a 6-week-old versus a 5-year-old actually needs from their day.

6 weeks – 12 months

Infant Program

Our nursery is a calm, low-stimulation space built around each baby's individual rhythm. With a strict 4:1 ratio, primary caregivers get to know every cry, coo, and comfort routine.

  • Individualized feeding, sleep & comfort routines
  • Daily tummy-time, sensory bins & soft music
  • Real-time app updates: naps, bottles, diapers
  • Hospital-grade sanitizing between every use
Enquire About Infant Care

A Typical Day

  • 7:30a Arrival, feeding & free floor play
  • 9:30a Tummy time & sensory exploration
  • 12:00p Nap, on each baby's own schedule
  • 3:30p Soft music & one-on-one bonding time

Key Learning Outcomes

Secure attachment Gross motor development Sensory awareness Early communication cues
1 – 2 years

Toddler Program

Newly mobile and endlessly curious, toddlers thrive on repetition, movement, and language. Our classrooms balance guided routines with plenty of open-ended play.

  • Vocabulary-building songs, books & conversation
  • Potty-training support, on your family's timeline
  • Gross-motor play: climbing, dancing, ball games
  • Early sharing & turn-taking social skills
Enquire About Toddler Care

A Typical Day

  • 7:30a Arrival & free play
  • 9:00a Circle time, songs & stories
  • 10:30a Outdoor play & gross-motor games
  • 1:00p Nap & quiet rest
  • 3:30p Art & sensory bins

Key Learning Outcomes

Vocabulary growth Independence in routines Turn-taking & sharing Gross motor coordination
3 – 4 years

Preschool Program

A play-based, standards-aligned curriculum introduces letters, numbers, science, and social-emotional skills through hands-on centers, not worksheets.

  • Letter recognition & early phonics
  • Counting, patterns & hands-on STEM stations
  • Daily circle-time & social-emotional check-ins
  • Weekly art, music & movement specials
Enquire About Preschool

A Typical Day

  • 7:30a Arrival & learning centers
  • 9:00a Circle time & calendar
  • 9:30a Letters, numbers & STEM stations
  • 10:30a Outdoor play
  • 3:00p Art, music & movement rotation

Key Learning Outcomes

Letter & number recognition Problem-solving skills Social-emotional regulation Creative expression
4 – 5 years

Pre-K Program

Our kindergarten-readiness track sharpens independence, early writing, and math reasoning, with individualized progress conferences each semester.

  • Kindergarten-readiness skill assessments
  • Name-writing, journaling & early math reasoning
  • Independence routines: self-serve meals, cubbies
  • Semester family conferences & portfolios
Enquire About Pre-K

A Typical Day

  • 7:30a Arrival & morning journaling
  • 9:00a Circle time & calendar math
  • 9:30a Reading & writing workshop
  • 11:00a STEM exploration & outdoor play
  • 3:00p Independent projects & portfolios

Key Learning Outcomes

Kindergarten-ready literacy Early math reasoning Self-directed independence Confident communication
