Classroom Highlights

Rooms Built Around the Age Inside Them

Every classroom is purpose-built for the children who use it — soft flooring and low shelving for infants, dramatic-play corners for toddlers, and standards-aligned learning centers for preschool and Pre-K. Natural light and neutral walls keep the focus on the kids, not the decor.

Outdoor Play Area

A Fenced Yard Kids Never Want to Leave

Our fully fenced playground has separate zones for climbing, running, and quiet outdoor play, all on impact-rated safety surfacing. Every child gets outdoor time daily — rain, shine, or first snow (with the right layers).

Arts & Crafts

Process Over Perfection

Our art studio is stocked with paint, clay, collage materials, and open-ended supplies that let kids experiment freely. We care more about the mixing, squishing, and decision-making than a picture-perfect result — and every masterpiece goes home at pickup.

  • Open art studio available daily
  • Seasonal & themed craft projects
  • Non-toxic, washable materials only
