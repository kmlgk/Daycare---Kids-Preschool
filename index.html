Welcome to Little Sprouts

A Second Home Where Every Child Feels Seen

For over 15 years, families in Brookfield Heights have trusted Little Sprouts to care for their children's earliest, most important years. Our play-based curriculum blends structured learning with plenty of time to imagine, build, and giggle.

Every classroom is led by degree-qualified teachers, kept to small group sizes, and filled with natural light, soft rugs, and hands-on learning centers designed just for growing minds.

  • Play-based curriculum
  • Chef-prepared meals
  • Live classroom webcams
  • Secure keycard entry

Programs by Age

A Program for Every Stage of Growing Up

Infant Program · 6 weeks – 12 months

A calm, sensory-rich nursery with a strict 4:1 caregiver ratio. We follow each family's feeding, sleep, and comfort routines closely, with daily digital reports so you never miss a milestone.

  • Individualized feeding & nap schedules
  • Tummy-time & sensory exploration
  • Daily photo & report app updates
View Full Curriculum

Toddler Program · 1 – 2 years

Curious walkers and talkers explore language, movement, and independence through guided play, music, and their first taste of structured group routines.

  • Language & vocabulary building
  • Potty-training support & routines
  • Gross-motor play & music time
View Full Curriculum

Preschool Program · 3 – 4 years

A play-based, standards-aligned curriculum covering early literacy, numbers, science, and social skills — building the confidence and habits kids need for kindergarten.

  • Letters, numbers & early literacy
  • Hands-on STEM & sensory centers
  • Social-emotional learning circles
View Full Curriculum

Pre-K Program · 4 – 5 years

Our kindergarten-readiness track sharpens writing, math reasoning, and independence, with a dedicated transition unit and family conferences each semester.

  • Kindergarten-readiness assessments
  • Early writing & math reasoning
  • Semester family conferences
